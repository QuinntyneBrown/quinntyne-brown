Scope picker

Which orgs a cross-org role covers: the portfolio, a type, or chosen orgs.

Overview

The scope picker chooses which orgs a cross-org role covers: the whole portfolio, every org of a type, or chosen orgs. The chosen option reveals its detail and a live summary of what it covers today and whether new orgs are covered automatically.

Scope

Covers 2 orgs today: FaithTech Toronto and FaithTech Austin. New FaithTech City orgs are covered automatically.

Scope

Covers 2 orgs today: FaithTech Toronto and FaithTech Austin. New FaithTech City orgs are covered automatically.

Anatomy

  1. GroupA fieldset with the legend “Scope”.
  2. Option cardA radio with a caption; bordered, tinted when chosen.
  3. DetailUnder the chosen option only: an Org Type select or org checkboxes.
  4. SummaryA polite live line: “Covers 2 orgs today… New FaithTech City orgs are covered automatically.”

Radio and checkbox states come from Radio group and Checkbox group. Invalid: chosen orgs with none ticked; the error is joined to the org group.

Responsive behaviour

Options stack at every width; the detail indents under the radio and wraps.

Theming

The chosen option uses --color-accent-subtle with an accent edge in both themes.

Accessibility

  • A radio group (Arrow keys move between the three scopes) followed by the chosen scope’s controls in reading order.
  • The summary is role="status", so a change of scope or type is announced once.
  • The chosen card is marked by the checked radio, not only by its tint.

Content

  • “Whole portfolio” — “Every org of {portfolio}, now and later”.
  • “Every org of a type” — “Follows the type as orgs are created”.
  • “Chosen orgs” — “A fixed list”; summary “Covers {n} orgs. Orgs created later aren’t covered.”
