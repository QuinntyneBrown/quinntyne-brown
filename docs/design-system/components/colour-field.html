Colour field

An org accent colour with a live contrast check against its foreground.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: new

Overview

The colour field sets an org’s accent. It pairs a native colour picker with a hex text field, previews the accent behind its foreground, and shows the measured contrast ratio. The foreground is white or #111111, whichever contrasts more; below 4.5:1 the field is invalid and the server refuses the save.

Aa FaithTech Toronto

Used behind your logo in the switcher and as the band on public event pages. Text on it is white or near-black, whichever reads better; it needs at least 4.5:1.

Anatomy

  1. Label“Accent colour (optional)”.
  2. SwatchA native input type="color" named “Pick the accent colour”; it mirrors the hex field and is skipped by Tab (the hex field is the keyboard path).
  3. Hex fieldMono, upper-case #RRGGBB.
  4. Preview“Aa {org}” on the accent pair.
  5. Ratio“Contrast with {white | near-black} text: 6.25:1” and a Passes AA or Below 4.5:1 badge.
  6. Help and errorJoined to the hex field.

Variants

One variant. The foreground choice is computed, not chosen.

Sizes

One size: 44px controls.

Below 4.5:1 shows both ratios, sets aria-invalid, and repeats the server’s message “This colour doesn't have enough contrast. Choose a darker or lighter shade.” in the error summary. Focus, hover and disabled come from Text field.

Responsive behaviour

Swatch, hex and preview sit in one row that wraps; at 360px the preview moves under the inputs. The ratio line wraps after the badge.

Theming

The field follows the theme; the preview shows the org pair exactly as it will look, which is the same in both themes.

Accessibility

  • The hex field is the labelled control; the ratio line, help and error are joined with aria-describedby, so the ratio is read with the field.
  • The ratio updates as a polite announcement after typing stops, not on every key.
  • The badge pairs a word with its colour.
  • The preview is aria-hidden; the ratio says the same thing in words.

Content

  • “Contrast with white text: 6.25:1 · Passes AA”.
  • Failing: “Contrast with near-black (#111111) text: 4.43:1 · 4.26:1 with white · Below 4.5:1”.
  • Error (from the API, L2-149): “This colour doesn't have enough contrast. Choose a darker or lighter shade.”
