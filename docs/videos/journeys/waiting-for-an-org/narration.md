# Waiting to be added

## opening: Opening
In this video you sign in with an account that does not belong to any organization yet. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## waiting: Waiting to be added
Joel has an account but no organization, so he lands on a page that says he is waiting to be added, and that his account is ready.

## profile: What he can do
There is no menu of organization pages, because there is nothing to show yet. He can still open his own profile, and sign out.

## added: Added to an organization
Once an Org Lead or the Portfolio Lead adds him, Check again takes him straight into FaithTech Toronto.

## closing: Summary
That is the waiting page: your account is ready before you have an organization, and Check again brings you in once someone adds you.
