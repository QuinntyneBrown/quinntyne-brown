# Manage organizations in the portfolio

## opening: Opening
In this video the Portfolio Lead manages the organizations in the portfolio. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## list: The organizations
The Portfolio section lists every organization with its type and its address. FaithTech Toronto is a FaithTech City; Shelfie is a Startup.

## create: Create an organization
New organization asks for a name, an address and an Org Type. The word all is reserved for the All orgs view, so the form refuses it and says why. Use a real address, pick FaithTech City, and the new organization appears in the list.

## archive: Archive it
Archiving asks first, then moves the organization out of the active list. Its data is kept.

## restore: Restore it
Open the Archived tab and restore it, and a message confirms the organization is back.

## closing: Summary
That is the portfolio's organization list: every organization in one place, new ones created with their own address, and archive and restore when they are not needed.
