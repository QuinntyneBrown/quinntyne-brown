# Cross-org roles

## opening: Opening
In this video the Portfolio Lead gives someone a role that works across organizations. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## roles: What they cover
A cross-org role applies one set of permissions across many organizations. Staff covers every FaithTech City organization, and can read but not change, other than the pages Staff own.

## assign: Assign Staff
Choose Assign Staff, pick Ruth Alvarez and confirm. A message says Ruth now holds Staff, and every FaithTech City organization shows up in her switcher.

## closing: Summary
That is a cross-org role: one assignment, many organizations, with coverage set by a list, a type or the whole portfolio.
