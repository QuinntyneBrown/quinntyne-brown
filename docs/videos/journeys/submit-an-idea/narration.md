# Submit an app idea

## opening: Opening
In this video you share an app idea with the community. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## the-problem: The problem
Anyone can open Submit an app idea, signed in or not. A visitor gets three steps. The first asks what problem you have noticed and who feels it most; the preview beside the form fills in as you go.

## your-idea: Your idea
Next you name the idea, give it a one-line pitch and say how it would work. Each field counts down the characters you have left.

## review: Review and submit
Review shows every answer with an Edit button. A visitor adds their name and email, then submits, and the confirmation promises an email if there are questions.

## signed-in: Signed in
Signed in, you submit under your own name. Depending on the Idea form, you also say how far along the idea is and who you need. Both steps are optional and can be skipped.

## idea-form: The Idea form
A System Admin turns those two steps on or off on the Idea form, and hears about every idea in the bell and by email.

## closing: Summary
That is Submit an app idea: a short wizard for anyone, two optional steps for members, and every idea delivered to System Admin.
