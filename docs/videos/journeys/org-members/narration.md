# Members of an organization

## opening: Opening
In this video an Org Lead looks after the members of their organization. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## members: Who belongs
Organization in the menu opens the member list, with each person's roles. Ruth is a Volunteer and Olivia is the Org Lead.

## add-unknown: No account yet
Add member takes an email address. If no account uses it, the dialog says so and points you to Send an invite instead.

## add: Add someone with an account
Use the email of someone who has signed up, choose the role Volunteer, and add them. Sami joins the list at once, with a message to confirm.

## remove: Remove a member
Removing asks first and says what happens: the person loses every role in the organization. Confirm, and Ruth is gone from the list.

## last-lead: The last Org Lead
Try to remove the only Org Lead and the app refuses, because every organization needs one. Cancel, and Olivia stays.

## closing: Summary
That is the member list: see who belongs, add people who have an account, and remove them, though never the last Org Lead.
