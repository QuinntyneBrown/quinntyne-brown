# Resources

## opening: Opening
In this video you find and open a resource. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## list: The list
Resources lists the decks, templates and links your organization shares. Each shows its title and type, and you can search or filter by tag.

## open: Open a resource
Open the city launch deck. Its page shows the file, who can see it, which here is everyone in the organization, and its owner, Grace.

## owner-actions: What the owner can do
Everyone gets Download. Because Grace owns the deck, she also gets Edit and Archive.

## closing: Summary
That is a resource: found in a list, opened to its details, owner and visibility, with edit and archive for the owner.
