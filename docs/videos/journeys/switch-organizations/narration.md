# Switch organizations

## opening: Opening
In this video you switch between organizations. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## current: Where you are
Pat covers two organizations, FaithTech Toronto and Shelfie. The top bar names the one you are in, and the menu shows the pages that organization has turned on, such as Events.

## menu: The switcher
Open the switcher to see every organization you cover, each with its type, and a tick beside the current one. At the bottom is All orgs.

## switch: Choose Shelfie
Choose Shelfie. The address changes to Shelfie, and the menu follows its features. Shelfie is a startup with no events, so Events leaves the menu and Workspaces stays.

## all-orgs: All orgs
Choose All orgs to see your work from every organization together. Pages that only make sense inside one organization drop away, and My work appears.

## closing: Summary
That is the switcher: one menu for every organization you cover, a navigation that follows each organization's features, and All orgs to bring it together.
