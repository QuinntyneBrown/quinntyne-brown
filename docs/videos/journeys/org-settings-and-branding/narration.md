# Organization settings and branding

## opening: Opening
In this video an Org Lead looks at their organization's settings and changes its branding. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## general: General
General shows the organization's name and its address. The Org Lead can read them, but only the Portfolio Lead changes them, and the page says so.

## branding: Branding
Branding is the Org Lead's. It starts with the display name people see in the top bar and on public pages.

## save: A new name and accent
Type a shorter display name and pick a green accent colour. The page measures the contrast against white text and says it passes. Save, and a message confirms it, and the preview band changes to match.

## low-contrast: Too pale
Try a pale grey and the page warns that it falls below four and a half to one. Save anyway and it is refused, with the measured ratio, so nobody ends up with text they cannot read.

## closing: Summary
That is branding: the name and address belong to the Portfolio Lead, the look belongs to the Org Lead, and every accent is checked for contrast.
