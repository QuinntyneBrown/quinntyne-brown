# L1 - High-Level Requirements

Sources: `idea.txt`, `decisions.txt` (wins on conflict) and `docs/mocks/`. The mock area tag follows each title.

QuinntyneBrown.Community is the community platform of Quinntyne Brown, Christian, Chief Product Officer and founder, and City Lead for FaithTech Toronto. It has the purpose of a FaithTech city community and is also a space for anyone and everyone Quinntyne works with or engages with; anyone may create an account.

## L1-001: Identity and accounts (AUTH)
The system shall let anyone create an account with a locally stored, securely hashed password, verify their email, sign in through OAuth2 authorization code + PKCE, sign out, and reset a forgotten password. Google and GitHub are external sign-in methods: a person may sign in or create an account with a Google account whose email Google has verified, or with a GitHub account whose primary email GitHub has verified.

## L1-002: Profiles and photos (PROF)
Every account shall have a profile with a name and up to ten photos, one of them featured, visible only to signed-in members.

## L1-003: Roles and role assignment (ROLE)
The system shall support multiple roles per profile and shall restrict who is permitted to grant or remove each role. Roles are held per organization and defined as data in each Org Type's catalogue, with System Admin, Portfolio Lead, Org Lead and Member built in (L1-025).

## L1-004: Invites (INV)
Members whose roles allow it, Org Leads and the Portfolio Lead shall be able to invite people by email into one organization, optionally pre-assigning a role, with invites that expire. People whose roles allow it shall also have a reusable personal QR code per organization that lets someone they meet join that organization and connect with them (L1-025).

## L1-005: Private contacts (CON)
Leads shall be able to create, search, annotate, photograph, archive, restore and permanently delete contacts that are private to them, see them as a grid of person cards, and link a contact to the person's real profile when they sign up, automatically when the person joins through the lead's QR code. A contact's photos are as private as the contact. A lead adding a contact shall be warned when one of their own contacts already has that email (L2-116).

## L1-006: Partner pipeline (PART)
Leads shall share one partner pipeline, shown as a Kanban board with the stages Lead, In Funnel and Confirmed. Leads shall log calls, emails, meetings and notes on a shared partner timeline, and shall be warned of and able to merge duplicate partners (L2-110 to L2-115).

## L1-007: City team (TEAM)
The system shall show the team: everyone who holds a lead or volunteer role.

## L1-008: Events (EVT)
Leads shall be able to create, edit and cancel events, members-only or public. Members and guests shall be able to RSVP, and every event shall offer an .ics download. Hackathon is an event type. Leads shall check attendees in at the door, add walk-ins and export attendance (L2-117 to L2-122).

## L1-009: Global search (SRCH)
Signed-in members shall be able to search profiles, events, partners, their own private contacts and the Agile work they can access, with results filtered by permission.

## L1-010: Notifications (NOTIF)
The system shall notify users in-app and by email about events that concern them.

## L1-011: Announcements (ANN)
Communications Leads shall be able to send announcements to everyone, to the holders of a role, or to an event's RSVP list. Recipients shall be able to opt out of announcement emails.

## L1-012: Agile team workspaces (AGILE)
City Leads shall be able to run their team's work in workspaces using Scrum or Kanban, with Initiatives, Epics, Stories and Tasks, a board, a backlog and sprints.

## L1-013: Planning Poker (AGILE)
Workspace members shall be able to estimate stories together in live Planning Poker sessions.

## L1-014: System administration and audit (ADMIN)
System Admins shall have full override, including suspending users and assigning any role. Every admin action shall be recorded in an audit log.

## L1-015: Security
The system shall enforce every authorization rule on the server, validate all input, and protect against the OWASP Top 10.

## L1-016: Accessibility and responsive design
Every screen shall meet WCAG 2.2 AA and work at viewport widths from extra small (<640px) to extra large (>=1280px), in light and dark themes.

## L1-017: Performance and reliability
The system shall respond quickly under expected load, degrade gracefully, and be observable in production.

## L1-018: Error and system pages (SYS)
The system shall show clear, recoverable pages for not found, forbidden, server error and offline conditions.

## L1-019: Home (HOME)
Every signed-in member shall land on a home page that summarizes what is coming up and what has happened, tailored to their roles.

## L1-020: Resources (RES)
Members whose roles allow it shall be able to add decks, logos, images, training materials and templates as uploaded files or https links, organised by type and tags, each private to its owner, shared with chosen members and roles, visible to the whole organization, or (set by the Portfolio Lead) to the whole portfolio (L1-025). Members shall be able to find, open and download the resources visible to them, and global search shall include them.

## L1-021: Marketing page (MKT)
An anonymous visitor at `www.faithtech.community` shall see a public marketing page that explains QuinntyneBrown.Community with a short brochure video and lets them sign in, sign up or sign in with Google without leaving the page, then lands them in the community app with a real session. The page shall also introduce Quinntyne Brown, whose community it is: their work, what they are building, their LinkedIn and GitHub, and their résumé to download.

## L1-022: Docs & notes (NOTES)
Signed-in members shall be able to write block-based pages, such as meeting notes, playbooks and how-tos, and format, link and nest them. Each page shall be private to its owner, shared with chosen members and roles, visible to the whole organization, or (set by the Portfolio Lead) to the whole portfolio (L1-025), and only its owner or a System Admin shall be able to change it. Members shall be able to find and read the pages visible to them, see which pages link to a page, and find pages through global search.

## L1-023: Private preview and deployment (WALL)
While the product is in private preview, the system shall be deployed to `app.faithtech.community` (community app, API and identity server) and `www.faithtech.community` (marketing page) over HTTPS behind a server-enforced wall: until a visitor unlocks it with the owner's passphrase, every request to either host shall be refused and the only page shown shall be a neutral unlock page that reveals nothing about the product. After unlocking, both hosts shall behave exactly as they do without the wall. The owner shall be able to rotate the passphrase, revoke every unlock and lift the wall by configuration.

## L1-024: Reports (RPT)
Holders of the reports permission shall see, for the organizations they cover (L1-025), a read-only report of city-level numbers (members, events held, RSVPs and check-ins, partners confirmed and announcements sent), filtered by city and period, with charts and tables they can export. Private contacts shall never be counted.

## L1-025: Organizations (ORG)
The system shall host several organizations under a portfolio. Every record shall belong to exactly one organization; members shall see their own organization's data, and the Portfolio Lead, cross-organization roles and System Admin shall see across the organizations they cover. Each organization shall be of an Org Type that defines its role catalogue and enabled features, where roles are data bundles of server-defined permission codes. Profiles shall be global and visible only to people who share or cover one of the person's organizations. People shall join an organization only through its invite or connect link, or by being added by its Org Lead or the Portfolio Lead. The app shall carry the active organization in the URL with a switcher and an "All orgs" view, public event pages shall be addressed by organization with light branding, and AI agents shall act as members with scoped, expiring, revocable tokens. Anything outside the caller's coverage, or in a feature switched off for the organization's type, shall be not found. Private contacts, resources and note pages shall remain visible only to their owner and System Admin.

## L1-026: Application integrations (INT)
Other applications, such as Goodwork, shall be able to push events to the system. An Org Lead or the Portfolio Lead shall register each application in one organization and receive a source ID and a signing secret shown once; the application shall sign every request with HMAC-SHA256, and the system shall reject anything unsigned, stale, oversized, invalid, from a revoked source or over its rate limit, and store each event once. Everyone whose coverage includes the organization (L1-025) shall see its applications and their events, newest first, with contact requests shown as name, email, message and received time. Secrets shall be stored encrypted, rotated with a 24-hour overlap and revoked permanently, and every register, rotate and revoke shall be audited.

## L1-027: Ideas (IDEA)
Anyone, signed in or not, shall be able to submit an app idea to the community through a short wizard at `/ideas/new`: the problem and who feels it, the idea's name, one-line pitch and how it works, then a review of their answers before they submit, with a live preview of the idea beside the form. A visitor who is not signed in shall give their name and email on the last step; a signed-in member's name and email shall come from their account, the idea shall be linked to their profile, and they shall also answer the optional steps "How far along is it?" (stage, platform, topics) and "Who do you need?" (skills, involvement, time) that a System Admin has turned on in the Idea form at `/admin/ideas`. Every idea shall belong to one platform intake in the portfolio's root organization (L1-025). Each submission shall notify every System Admin in the app and by email with the whole idea, and send the submitter a confirmation email. The anonymous submit endpoint shall be validated and rate limited on the server (L1-015). Answers shall live only in the open page: there are no saved drafts. Out of scope, as a follow-up feature: a screen for leads or System Admins to browse, review or triage submitted ideas; ideas are stored so it can list them later (L2-168 to L2-181).
